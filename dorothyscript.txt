DOROTHY TOURNAMENT PRESENTATION SCRIPT
Target time: about 1 minute 45 seconds.

Rehearse the ideas, but do not read this script during the presentation. The individual rubric caps
the verbal category at 3 out of 5 when notes are read. Use each slide as a prompt and speak to the
audience.


SLIDE 13 OF 30 - TOURNAMENT

"My user story is: As a player, I want to run an eight-entrant knockout tournament so that I can
see one champion. These screenshots show the before, during, and after views. The player chooses or
randomizes entrants, four quarterfinals run together, and winners advance through eight, four, two,
and one."


SLIDE 14 OF 30 - TOURNAMENT INTERACTOR

"RunTournamentRoundInteractor is the Use Case Interactor. Its responsibility is to coordinate one
round: load the tournament, pair entrants, ask a match runner for each result, record the winners,
save, and present. The Tournament entity protects bracket order and valid progression. I am using
the code only to identify the Interactor's role, not to walk through each line."


SLIDE 15 OF 30 - TOURNAMENT UML

"The UML maps the use case to the four Clean Architecture layers. React, HTTP, Spring, and the
Executor are Frameworks and Drivers. Controllers and the Presenter are Interface Adapters. The
Interactor is an Application Business Rule. Tournament, TournamentRound, and TournamentMatch are
Entities. The Interactor depends on interfaces owned by its use-case layer, so dependencies point
inward."


SLIDE 16 OF 30 - TOURNAMENT DESIGN

"TournamentMatchRunner is a Strategy. RandomBattleRunner is the production implementation, and
tests replace it with a fixed runner. RandomBattleRunner calls Albert's ResolveTurnInputBoundary,
so tournament reuses the existing single-battle rules rather than copying them. A new Battle is
created for each matchup, resetting HP, and an injected Executor lets matches in one round run in
parallel."
